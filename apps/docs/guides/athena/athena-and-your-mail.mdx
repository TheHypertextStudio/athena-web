## Athena's own address

Athena has an inbox. Forward anything to it — a confirmation, a thread you need to act on, a
document someone sent you — and it becomes context she can work from.

A received message is stored as a **context object** rather than as an email. It carries a title,
its content, where it came from, and when — the same shape any other piece of context has. That
is what lets a message be attached to work without dragging an email client's worth of concepts
along with it.

Nothing about this depends on connecting a mailbox. It has no relationship to the mail Docket
syncs out of a connected account.

## Mail into tasks

Connect Gmail and the pipeline runs the other direction: Athena reads what arrives, works out
what work it implies, and drafts a Task with the original email **attached as context**.

**The task is the real object; the email is an attachment.** You act on the task, and the message
that caused it stays one click away for when you need to check what was actually said.

### It is always a suggestion first

A drafted task is a [proposal](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals), not a task. It waits for
you to accept, edit, or dismiss it. Nothing is created behind your back.

Each suggestion carries a confidence indicator and a preview of the source thread, so deciding
usually takes a glance.

### Dates come from the message, never from a guess

If an email states a date, the drafted task carries it. If it does not, the task has no due date.
Athena declines to infer one, because a confidently wrong deadline is worse than none.

## Rules and automations

Once suggestions are flowing you can automate the obvious parts. Rules act on incoming suggestions
and on tasks, and can:

- Set a status, assignee, or priority
- Apply a label
- Send a notification
- Accept a suggestion automatically

Enabling email-to-task seeds a sensible default set of rules immediately, so it is useful before
you configure anything.

Set this up in **Settings → Connections → Email to task**, and manage rules in
**Settings → Automations**.

## Housekeeping

Suggestions you never act on expire after 30 days, and resolved ones are cleared after 90. The
queue stays a queue rather than becoming an archive.

## When mail access breaks

If the connection loses its authorization, it is flagged as needing re-authorization and you are
notified. A mail connector never reports success while reading nothing.

Re-authorize from **Settings → Connections**.
