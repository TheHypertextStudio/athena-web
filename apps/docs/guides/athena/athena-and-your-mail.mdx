Forward a thread to Athena's own address, or connect Gmail and let her draft the tasks your mail
implies. Either works without the other.

## Forward it to her

Athena has an email address. Forward the venue confirmation, the thread you owe a reply to, or the
budget someone sent you, and she works from it.

Docket stores the message with a title, its content, the sender, and the date, then attaches it to
whatever work you point it at.

## Turn Gmail into tasks

Connect Gmail and Athena drafts a Task from what arrives, with the original email attached. Work
the task. Open the message when you need to check what the caterer said.

### Accept it before it is a task

Every drafted task is a [proposal](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals). Accept it, edit it, or
dismiss it. Docket creates no task until you accept one.

Each suggestion shows a confidence indicator and a preview of the source thread.

### Dates come from the message

An email that says "by the 14th" gives the task a due date of the 14th. An email that names no
date gives the task no due date. Athena declines to infer one.

## Automation rules

A rule can:

- Set a status, assignee, or priority
- Apply a label
- Send a notification
- Accept a suggestion automatically

Turning on email-to-task seeds a default set of rules.

Turn it on in **Settings → Connections → Email to task**. Edit the rules in
**Settings → Automations**.

## Suggestions expire after 30 days

Suggestions you never act on expire after 30 days. Resolved ones clear after 90.

## When mail access breaks

Docket flags the connection as needing re-authorization and notifies you. A mail connector never
reports success while reading nothing. Re-authorize in **Settings → Connections**.
