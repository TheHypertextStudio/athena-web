## Athena's own address

Athena has an inbox. Forward anything to it, such as a confirmation, a thread you need to act on,
or a document someone sent you, and it becomes context she can work from.

Docket stores a received message as a **context object**. It carries a title, its content, where
it came from, and when, which is the shape every other piece of context has. You can attach the
message to work without bringing email-specific concepts along with it.

This works whether or not you connect a mailbox, and it is independent of the mail Docket syncs
out of a connected account.

## Mail into tasks

Connect Gmail and Athena reads what arrives, works out what work it implies, and drafts a Task
with the original email **attached as context**.

**The task is the real object, and the email is an attachment.** You act on the task, and the
message that caused it stays one click away for when you need to check what was actually said.

### Every drafted task starts as a suggestion

A drafted task is a [proposal](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals). It waits for you to
accept, edit, or dismiss it. Docket creates no task until you accept one.

Each suggestion carries a confidence indicator and a preview of the source thread, so deciding
usually takes a glance.

### Dates come from the message

If an email states a date, the drafted task carries it. If the email states no date, the task has
no due date. Athena declines to infer one, because a confidently wrong deadline does more damage
than a missing one.

## Rules and automations

Once suggestions are flowing, you can automate the obvious parts. Rules act on incoming
suggestions and on tasks, and can:

- Set a status, assignee, or priority
- Apply a label
- Send a notification
- Accept a suggestion automatically

Enabling email-to-task seeds a sensible default set of rules immediately, so it is useful before
you configure anything.

Set this up in **Settings → Connections → Email to task**, and manage rules in
**Settings → Automations**.

## Housekeeping

Suggestions you never act on expire after 30 days, and resolved ones clear after 90 days. The
queue stays short enough to work through.

## When mail access breaks

If the connection loses its authorization, Docket flags it as needing re-authorization and
notifies you. A mail connector never reports success while reading nothing.

Re-authorize from **Settings → Connections**.
