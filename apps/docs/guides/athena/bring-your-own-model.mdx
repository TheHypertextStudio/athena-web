---
title: "Bring your own model"
description: "Run Athena's model work on a computer you own, so the prompt and your workspace context never leave it."
---

You can point Athena's model work at a machine you own. Her replies are then generated there
instead of on Docket's model service.

The prompt, the workspace context, and the reply stay on a computer you control, so you can say
exactly where your data went.

## What you need

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Lovelace account">
    Lovelace Lattice is the compute network this runs on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A paired machine">
    A laptop or desktop running the Lattice daemon, which fronts a local model server such as
    Ollama or LM Studio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Docket">
    From **Settings → Athena**, connect your Lovelace account and pick which of your paired
    machines to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, Athena's model work runs there.

## How it reaches your machine

Your computer **polls outward**. You open no inbound port, run no tunnel, and accept no callback
from the cloud into your home network.

Work travels through Lovelace's hosted gateway. Docket sends the request to the gateway, your
daemon picks it up on its next poll, runs it locally, and the answer returns the same way.

<Note>
  **Docket never talks to your device.** It talks to the gateway. Your machine stays unaddressable
  from the internet, and this setup never needs it to be otherwise.
</Note>

## What changes, and what does not

| Where it runs | What runs there |
| --- | --- |
| Your machine | Generating Athena's replies, which is the model work |
| Docket | Your work itself: tasks, projects, comments, calendar, the time ledger |

Athena's behaviour is unchanged. Sessions, proposals, approvals, and permissions all work exactly
as they do otherwise. See [Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## When the machine is off

A closed laptop cannot answer. Docket tells you so plainly and never falls back to a hosted model
without saying it did, so you always know where a reply came from.

## Setting it up

**Settings → Athena** holds the connection, the machine picker, and the model selection. Disconnect
at any time to return Athena's model work to Docket's own service.
