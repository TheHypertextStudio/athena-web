---
title: "Bring your own model"
description: "Point Athena's model work at a computer you own, so the prompt and your workspace context never leave it."
---

You can point Athena's model work at a machine you own. Her replies are then generated there
instead of on Docket's model service.

The reason to want this is that **"where did my data go" becomes answerable**. The prompt, the
workspace context, and the reply never leave a computer you control.

## What you need

<Steps>
  <Step title="A Lovelace account">
    Lovelace Lattice is the compute network this runs on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A paired machine">
    A laptop or desktop running the Lattice daemon, which fronts a local model server — Ollama or
    LM Studio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Docket">
    From **Settings → Athena**, connect your Lovelace account and pick which of your paired
    machines to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, Athena's model work runs there.

## How it reaches your machine

Your computer **polls outward**. There is no inbound port to open, no tunnel to run, and no
callback from the cloud into your home network.

Work travels through Lovelace's hosted gateway: Docket sends the request to the gateway, your
daemon picks it up on its next poll, runs it locally, and the answer comes back the same way.

<Note>
  **Docket never talks to your device.** It talks to the gateway. Your machine is never addressable
  from the internet, and nothing about this setup requires it to be.
</Note>

## What changes, and what does not

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Runs on your machine | Generating Athena's replies — the model work |
| Stays in Docket | Your work itself: tasks, projects, comments, calendar, the time ledger |

Everything about how Athena behaves is unchanged. Sessions, proposals, approvals, and permissions
all work exactly as they do otherwise — see
[Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## When the machine is off

A laptop that is closed cannot answer. Docket surfaces that plainly rather than silently falling
back to a hosted model without telling you, so you always know where a reply came from.

## Setting it up

**Settings → Athena** holds the connection, the machine picker, and the model selection. You can
disconnect at any time, which returns Athena's model work to Docket's own service.
