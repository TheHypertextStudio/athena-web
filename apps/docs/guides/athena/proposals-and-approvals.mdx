---
title: "Proposals and approvals"
description: "Where Docket shows an agent's work before it becomes real, and how you say yes."
---

Athena shows a proposed task as a faded copy with a dashed border and a `proposed` badge, in the
same place Docket would show the real one. Approve it and it becomes real, in that spot.

Edit the copy before you approve it. Change the date, change the assignee, change the title.
Docket creates exactly what you approved.

## Approve from wherever you are

Docket shows the same approval in three places, and acting in one clears it everywhere:

- The **session**, beside the work it came from.
- Your [**Inbox**](/guides/daily/inbox), across every workspace.
- [**Home**](/guides/daily/home-today), inline with today's plan.

## Approve, edit, or reject

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Approve | The proposal becomes the real object, in place. |
| Edit, then approve | Docket creates what you edited. |
| Reject | Docket discards the proposal and gives the session your reason. |

Approve eleven proposed changes at once, or take them one at a time.

## Access and sign-off

**What an agent may touch.** Every agent starts **read-only** and asks for more access at the
moment it needs it, with the reason in front of you.

**Whether it needs sign-off.**

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `suggest` | Proposes only, and applies nothing on its own. |
| `apply after approval` | Waits for a yes on each change, then applies it. |
| `apply directly` | Acts, and records everything for review afterwards. |

The two settings are independent. Set a capable agent to `suggest`, or let a read-only agent apply
directly inside its narrow scope.

## Who approves

Whoever assigned or delegated the work approves it by default. A workspace or a team can change
that and name one person to sign off on agent work.

## Owner and delegate

Assign the task to yourself and name Athena the **delegate**. Docket records both names on every
action.

## Suggestions are not tasks

Accept a suggestion and Docket creates the task at that moment. Until then it is a proposal. The
same applies to [suggestions drafted from your mail](/guides/athena/athena-and-your-mail).
