Athena shows a proposed task as a faded copy of the task, sitting where the real one would sit,
with a dashed border and a `proposed` badge. Approve it and it becomes real, in that spot.

Edit the copy before you approve it. Change the date, change the assignee, change the title. What
you approve is what lands.

## Approve from wherever you are

The same approval waits in three places, and acting in one clears it everywhere:

- The **session**, beside the work it belongs to.
- Your [**Inbox**](/guides/daily/inbox), across every workspace.
- [**Home**](/guides/daily/home-today), inline with today's plan.

## Approve, edit, or reject

| Action | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Approve | The proposal becomes the real object, in place. |
| Edit, then approve | What you approve is what lands. |
| Reject | It goes away, and the session continues knowing why. |

Approve eleven proposed changes at once, or take them one at a time.

## Access and sign-off

**What an agent may touch.** Every agent starts **read-only** and asks for more access at the
moment it needs it, with the reason in front of you.

**Whether it needs sign-off.**

| Mode | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `suggest` | Proposes only, and applies nothing on its own. |
| `apply after approval` | Waits for a yes on each change, then applies it. |
| `apply directly` | Acts, and records everything for review afterwards. |

The two settings move independently. Hold a capable agent at `suggest`, or let a read-only agent
apply directly inside its narrow scope.

## Who approves

Whoever assigned or delegated the work approves it by default. A workspace or a team can change
that and name one person to sign off on agent work.

## Owner and delegate

Keep your name on the task and make Athena the **delegate**. You stay the assignee. Every action
records both names.

## Suggestions are not tasks

Accept a suggestion and Docket creates the task at that moment. Until then it is a proposal. The
same holds for [suggestions drafted from your mail](/guides/athena/athena-and-your-mail).
