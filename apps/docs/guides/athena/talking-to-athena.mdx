Natural language is Athena's primary medium. There is no command syntax to learn.

## Where to reach her

| Surface | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| The prompt box on [Home](/guides/daily/home-today) | Planning the day, quick questions, capturing something |
| Her own screen | Longer conversations that carry context across several turns |
| Assigning her a task | Work you want done, with a session you can watch |
| Her [email address](/guides/athena/athena-and-your-mail) | Forwarding things from outside Docket |

The Home prompt box takes a task and a question in the same input. Type an instruction and it
captures work. Ask something and you get an answer.

## What she can see

Athena reads the same work you can, under your own permissions, across every workspace you belong
to. She cannot see work you cannot see.

A conversation can be **focused** on one workspace so she knows which "the launch" you mean. Focus
tells her what you are talking about and leaves her permissions exactly as they were.

## What to ask her

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Planning">
    - "What should I focus on today?"
    - "Plan my day around the two calls I already have."
    - "I have four hours this afternoon. What's the best use of it?"
    - "Create a plan to make sure I get more sleep."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Finding out">
    - "What's blocking the Spring Gala project?"
    - "Which projects haven't had an update in two weeks?"
    - "What did I actually spend last week on?"
    - "Who's waiting on me?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Doing">
    - "Reschedule everything on Thursday afternoon."
    - "Draft thank-you notes for everyone who gave over $500."
    - "Break this project into tasks and estimate each one."
    - "File everything in Triage into the right project."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Looking back">
    - "Write the retrospective for this cycle."
    - "Summarise what changed on the nonprofit this month."
    - "How accurate were my estimates last week?"
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## She asks when she is unsure

Athena stops and asks instead of guessing. The question appears in the session and also reaches
your [Inbox](/guides/daily/inbox), so you can see a waiting session from wherever you are.

Answering resumes the work from where it paused.

## Steering mid-session

You can act on a session while it runs:

- Read the running account of what she is doing.
- Correct her direction in plain language.
- Approve or reject an individual proposed change.
- Take the work back and finish it yourself.

## Being specific helps

Athena works best from clear constraints.

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| "Sort out my week" | "Schedule my unscheduled tasks around my existing meetings, nothing after 6pm" |
| "Update the project" | "Post an update on Spring Gala saying we're at risk because the venue hasn't confirmed" |
| "Fix my tasks" | "Everything in Triage from Linear belongs in the Website Rebuild project" |

## Preferences

**Settings → Athena** holds how she works with you: tone, how much she asks before acting, and
what she takes initiative on.
