---
title: "What Athena is"
description: "Docket's built-in agent, who takes work in plain language and shows you what she does with it."
---

Assign a task to Athena and watch her work. Ask her in a sentence: "Reschedule the offsite."
"Create a plan to make sure I get more sleep."

## She belongs to you

**Athena belongs to your account, never to a workspace.** Focus a session on a workspace so she
knows which "the launch" you mean. That focus grants her no access. She resolves your identity and
your permissions in the workspace she acts in, on every action.

<Note>
  Athena can never do something you could not do yourself. Approving one of her proposals cannot
  supply authority you lack. Lose access to a project and she loses it on her next action.
</Note>

## Assign her work like a person

Docket calls a person and an agent the same thing: an **Actor**. Put Athena in the assignee field
where you would put a colleague.

## Watch the session

Every job Athena does opens a **session**.

```
You delegate a task  ─►  a session opens  ─►  you watch, steer, approve, or take over
```

Read her running account of what she is reading and what she is about to do. Correct her in a
sentence, answer the question she stops on, or take the work back and finish it yourself. Catch
the wrong turn while it still costs nothing.

A session drafting donor thank-yous behaves the same as one rebuilding next quarter's budget.

## Access and sign-off

Two settings move separately.

**What she may touch.** She starts **read-only** and asks for more access at the moment she needs
it, with the reason attached.

**Whether she needs sign-off.** **Suggest** proposes only. **Apply after approval** waits for your
yes. **Apply directly** acts and records everything.

Whoever assigned or delegated the work approves it by default. A workspace or a team can change
that. See [Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## Accountability

Docket records two names on every agent action: Athena, and the person who set her going, as
"Athena, on behalf of you". Her hours land in the [time ledger](/guides/daily/time) beside yours.

## Instructions she follows

Write standing instructions at the **workspace** or **team** level. Team instructions override the
workspace's.

- "Always draft, never send, without my OK."
- "Never assign work to volunteers without asking me first."

Set your own preferences in **Settings → Athena**.
