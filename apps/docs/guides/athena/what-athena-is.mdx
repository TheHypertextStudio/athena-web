---
title: "What Athena is"
description: "Docket's built-in agent, who takes work in plain language and shows you what she does with it."
---

Athena is Docket's own agent. You talk to her in plain language, hand her work the same way you
hand work to a person, and watch what she does while she does it.

She covers everything you manage in Docket. "Reschedule the offsite" and "create a plan to make
sure I get more sleep" are both fair things to ask.

## She belongs to you

**Athena belongs to your account, never to a workspace.** A session may be *focused* on a
workspace so she knows what you are talking about, and that focus grants her no access. Every
action she takes resolves *your* identity and *your* permissions in the workspace she is acting
in.

<Note>
  Athena can never do something you could not do yourself. Approving one of her proposals cannot
  supply authority you lack. If your access changes, hers changes with it on her next action.
</Note>

She assists you across every workspace you belong to, and she holds membership in none of them.

## Agents are how work gets done

Docket treats a person and an agent as the same kind of thing: an **Actor**. Everywhere the app
asks "who", it accepts either.

Delegating therefore uses the ordinary flow. You assign a task to Athena in the field where you
would assign it to a colleague, on the same screen, with the same gesture. Your views look
identical whichever you pick.

## Sessions are the unit

The **session** is the first-class object: one episode of an agent doing one job.

```
You delegate a task  ─►  a session opens  ─►  you watch, steer, approve, or take over
```

A session shows a live, plain-English account of what she is reading, what she is deciding, and
what she is about to do. You can read along, redirect her when she heads the wrong way, answer a
question when she is unsure, or take the work back entirely.

Because the account runs live, you catch a wrong turn while correcting it still costs nothing.

## Access and sign-off

Docket owns the safety model independently of how capable any agent is, and it has two settings
that move separately.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="What she may touch" icon="key">
    An agent starts **read-only** and asks for more access at the moment it needs it. You see the
    reason before you grant it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Whether actions need sign-off" icon="circle-check">
    **Suggest** proposes only. **Apply after approval** waits for your yes. **Apply directly** acts
    and records everything.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

By default the approver is whoever assigned or delegated the work. A workspace or a team can
change that. See [Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## Accountability

Docket attributes every agent action twice: to the agent that did it, and to the person who set it
in motion, recorded as "Athena, on behalf of you". You can always tell who stood behind a change.

Agent effort also lands in the [time ledger](/guides/daily/time) alongside human effort, so "how
long did this take" has one answer regardless of who did it.

## Instructions she follows

Written guidance set at the **workspace** and **team** level applies automatically, and team
instructions override the workspace's.

- "Always draft, never send, without my OK."
- "Match our brand voice."
- "Never assign work to volunteers without asking me first."

Your own preferences for how she works with you live in **Settings → Athena**.

## The same experience for every kind of work

A session drafting donor thank-you notes, a session updating next quarter's budget line by line,
and a session rescheduling a week of meetings all look and behave the same. Nothing in the
experience is specific to one kind of work.
