---
title: 'Cycles'
description: 'Plan the recurring week or two that a team works in without changing each task’s project or owner.'
---

Cycles divide a Team's work into recurring planning periods. Each Team chooses its cadence in Team
settings. One Team can use one-week Cycles while another uses two weeks.

## Let Docket maintain the cadence

Docket creates past, current, and upcoming Cycles on the Team's schedule and marks the current one
from today's date. You do not create or close each Cycle by hand.

## Move the plan without moving the work

Adding a Task to a Cycle changes when the Team plans to do it. The Task keeps its Project, Team,
assignee, and relationships.

A Cycle compares committed work with Team capacity and shows a burn-up of completed work. A
Project's work list defaults to the current Cycle.

Use a [Milestone](/guides/concepts/milestones) for a one-time checkpoint inside one Project.
