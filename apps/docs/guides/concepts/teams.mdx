A Team is a named group of people inside a workspace. Teams are the units that own work: each one
carries its own workflow states, its own cycle cadence, and its own triage queue.

Larger workspaces usually run several. A workspace with one team is completely normal, and a
personal space needs to think about teams roughly never.

## What a team owns

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Workflow states" icon="list-check">
    The stages work moves through in this team's words. Two teams in the same workspace can run
    different processes. See [Labels and statuses](/guides/concepts/labels-and-statuses).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cycles" icon="rotate">
    Each team runs its own cadence. One team's two-week cycle has nothing to do with another's
    monthly one. See [Cycles](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Triage" icon="inbox">
    Where work arrives before anyone has filed it — captured notes, items from a connected tool,
    anything without a home yet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## You cannot assign work to a team

This one catches people. Assignment always points at a single **Actor** — one person or one
agent. A Team is a grouping for organizing and for permissions.

The reason is accountability. "The team is on it" is how work goes quietly unowned; naming one
Actor keeps the answer to "who is doing this" short.

If you want work to belong to a team without belonging to a person yet, leave it unassigned in
that team's triage queue. That is a real, supported state.

## Teams and tasks

Every Task belongs to a workspace **and** a team. That is what gives it a set of workflow states
to move through and a triage queue to wait in.

A task's project is optional; its team is not.

## Teams and permissions

A team is a valid target for a [grant](/guides/concepts/workspaces#membership-roles-and-access),
so you can hand a role `contribute` on one team and `view` on another. The grant cascades down
into the work that team owns.

## Setting them up

Create and manage teams in **Settings → Members & Access**. Each team's workflow states live in
**Settings → Statuses**, and its cycle cadence in the team's own settings.

<Tip>
  Resist creating a team per project. Teams are for groups of people who share a process; the
  bounded piece of work is a [Project](/guides/concepts/projects).
</Tip>
