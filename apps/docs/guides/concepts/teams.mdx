---
title: "Teams"
description: "The units that own work, each with its own workflow, cycles, and triage queue."
---

A Team is a named group of people inside a workspace. Teams own work: each one carries its own
workflow states, its own cycle cadence, and its own triage queue.

Larger workspaces usually run several teams. A workspace with one team is completely normal, and a
personal space rarely needs to think about teams at all.

## What a team owns

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Workflow states" icon="list-check">
    The stages work moves through in this team's words. Two teams in the same workspace can run
    different processes. See [Labels and statuses](/guides/concepts/labels-and-statuses).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cycles" icon="rotate">
    Each team runs its own cadence. One team's two-week cycle is independent of another's monthly
    one. See [Cycles](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Triage" icon="inbox">
    Where work arrives before anyone has filed it: captured notes, items from a connected tool,
    and anything still unsorted.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## You cannot assign work to a team

Assignment always points at a single **Actor**, meaning one person or one agent. A Team is a
grouping for organizing work and for permissions.

Assigning to one Actor keeps accountability clear, so every task has a short answer to "who is
doing this".

If you want work to belong to a team before it belongs to a person, leave it unassigned in that
team's triage queue. Docket supports that state.

## Teams and tasks

Every Task belongs to a workspace **and** a team. The team gives the task a set of workflow states
to move through and a triage queue to wait in.

A task's project is optional. Its team is required.

## Teams and permissions

A team is a valid target for a [grant](/guides/concepts/workspaces#membership-roles-and-access),
so you can hand a role `contribute` on one team and `view` on another. The grant cascades down
into the work that team owns.

## Setting them up

Create and manage teams in **Settings → Members & Access**. Each team's workflow states live in
**Settings → Statuses**, and its cycle cadence in the team's own settings.

<Tip>
  Create a team for a group of people who share a process. For a bounded piece of work, create a
  [Project](/guides/concepts/projects) instead.
</Tip>
