---
title: "Updates and health"
description: "How you report what is really going on, and how that report becomes a signal other people can read at a glance."
---

An **Update** is a narrative status post on a Project, Program, or Initiative. You write what is
actually happening in your own words, and you set a verdict alongside it.

That verdict becomes the item's **health**, which everyone else reads from
[Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio) without opening the item.

## The three verdicts

| Health | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `on_track` | It is going the way it should. |
| `at_risk` | Something is threatening it. Nothing has gone wrong yet. |
| `off_track` | It has already slipped. |

A person asserts health. Docket never infers it from ticket counts or burn-down slope, because a
project can be 80% complete and failing, or 20% complete and completely fine.

## Health and progress

A Project reports **weighted progress** as well as health, and the two answer different questions.

| Signal | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Progress | How much of the work is done. |
| Health | Whether the remaining work is going to land. |

A reader needs both. A Program has no meaningful progress percentage, because it is designed to
keep going, so health and flow carry the whole signal there.

## Writing a good update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what changed">
    Cover the period since the last update, not since the beginning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what is in the way">
    Name the blocker and who owns it. State anything you are waiting on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the verdict honestly">
    An `at_risk` posted early helps everyone. An `on_track` posted the week before a slip erodes
    trust in the whole signal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write for the reader who is away from the work day to day and last looked a fortnight ago.
</Tip>

## Rolled-up health

An Initiative reports health rolled up from the Programs and Projects associated with it, so a
theme going wrong is visible from the top without anyone assembling a report.

Because of that roll-up, an honest `off_track` on a single project changes what every item above
it reports.

## Where updates show up

- On the item itself, as a chronological record of how it went.
- In [Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio), as the health chip on every row.
- In the [Inbox](/guides/daily/inbox) of people following the item.
- On a published **brief**, when the project is published.

## Attention signals

Initiatives raise their own attention flags when something needs looking at: `off_track`,
`at_risk`, or **stale**. Stale means nobody has posted an update in long enough that the health
signal can no longer be trusted.

Docket treats staleness as a signal in its own right, so an absent update never counts as a good
one.
