---
title: "Updates and health"
description: "A paragraph saying how it is going, plus one of three verdicts everyone else reads."
---

Post an Update on a Project, Program, or Initiative to say how it is going, and set one of three
verdicts with it. That verdict sets the item's **health**, which everyone else reads from
[Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio).

## The three verdicts

| Health | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `on_track` | It is going the way it should. |
| `at_risk` | Something is threatening it. Nothing has gone wrong yet. |
| `off_track` | It has already slipped. |

You set health. Docket never infers it from ticket counts or burn-down slope. The Spring Gala can
be 80% complete and failing.

## Writing a good update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what changed">
    Cover the period since your last update.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what is in the way">
    Name the blocker and who owns it. Name what you are waiting on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the verdict honestly">
    Post `at_risk` early and everyone can help. An `on_track` posted the week before a slip erodes
    trust in the whole signal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write for the reader who last looked a fortnight ago.
</Tip>

## Where your verdict travels

- The item's own record, in order.
- [Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio), as the health chip on every row.
- The [Inbox](/guides/daily/inbox) of anyone following the item.
- The published **brief**, when the project is published.

An Initiative rolls up health from every Program and Project linked to it. Post one honest
`off_track` on "Fall term enrolment" and "Grow revenue" reports it too.

## Attention flags

Initiatives raise their own flags: `off_track`, `at_risk`, or **stale**. Stale means nobody has
posted an update in long enough to trust the health signal. An absent update never counts as a good
one.
