## Your Hub

You own one Hub personally. No organization owns it. It reads from every workspace you belong to
and has four screens:

- [**Home**](/guides/daily/home-today): today's plan, your calendar, and what needs attention.
- [**Inbox**](/guides/daily/inbox): everything awaiting a response, from every workspace.
- [**Portfolio**](/guides/daily/portfolio): every workspace's programs and projects on one
  timeline.
- [**Search**](/guides/daily/search-and-views): one palette that searches, navigates, acts, and
  switches workspaces.

Every item on those screens shows the workspace it came from.

## A workspace

A workspace separates one venture from another. Docket shows its members, connected tools, agents,
[terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology), and work only inside it. Open one and the whole app
switches to it.

<Note>
  Docket never merges data across workspaces. Your Hub only reads from them. The same workspace
  appears in every member's own Hub, because the workspace is shared and no single Hub owns it.
</Note>

## Your personal space

Docket creates a personal space for you when you sign up as an individual. Tasks, projects,
cycles, and Athena work there exactly as they do in a shared workspace. It has no member roster
and no publishing settings, and it reads from your personal connections.

## Membership, roles, and access

A capability plus a grant decides access inside a workspace.

**Capabilities** form a single ordered list. A higher one satisfies every lower one.

| Capability | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| `view` | Read the item. |
| `comment` | Add comments and discussion. |
| `contribute` | Create and edit content such as tasks and fields. |
| `assign` | Route and assign work to others. |
| `manage` | Full control, including settings, membership, and deletion. |

**Grants** give a capability to one person or to everyone with a given role. Grant it on the whole
workspace, a team, an Initiative, a Program, a Project, a Cycle, or one task. A grant applies to
everything inside the item you grant it on. Give someone `contribute` on a Program and they get it
on every Project and Task in that Program.

Mark an item **public** to workspace members, or **private**, which only people with an explicit
grant can see.

Define your own roles per workspace: a nonprofit names one "Volunteer", an agency names one
"Client". Manage all of this in **Settings → Members & Access**.

## Switching, adding, and leaving

Switch and create workspaces from the switcher at the top of the sidebar. Leave one and Docket
removes it from your Hub. The workspace continues for everyone else.
