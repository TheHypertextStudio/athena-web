## Your Hub

You own one Hub personally. No organization owns it. It pulls from every workspace you belong to
and holds four screens:

- [**Home**](/guides/daily/home-today): today's plan, your calendar, and what needs attention.
- [**Inbox**](/guides/daily/inbox): everything awaiting a response, from every workspace.
- [**Portfolio**](/guides/daily/portfolio): every workspace's programs and projects on one
  timeline.
- [**Search**](/guides/daily/search-and-views): one palette that searches, navigates, acts, and
  switches workspaces.

Every item on those screens carries a chip naming the workspace it came from.

## A workspace

A workspace keeps one venture separate from another. Its members, connected tools, agents,
[terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology), and work all stay inside it, invisible from anywhere
else. Open one and the whole app switches to it.

<Note>
  Docket never merges data across workspaces. Your Hub only reads from them. The same workspace
  appears in every member's own Hub, because the workspace is shared and no single Hub owns it.
</Note>

## Your personal space

Docket creates a personal space for you when you sign up as an individual. Tasks, projects,
cycles, and Athena work there exactly as they do in a shared workspace. It carries no member
roster and no publishing settings, and it draws its data from your personal connections.

## Membership, roles, and access

A capability plus a grant decides access inside a workspace.

**Capabilities** form a single ordered list. A higher one satisfies every lower one.

| Capability | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| `view` | Read the item. |
| `comment` | Add comments and discussion. |
| `contribute` | Create and edit content such as tasks and fields. |
| `assign` | Route and assign work to others. |
| `manage` | Full control, including settings, membership, and deletion. |

**Grants** give one person, or everyone holding a role, a capability somewhere. Grant it on the
whole workspace, a team, an Initiative, a Program, a Project, a Cycle, or one task. A grant reaches
everything inside what you granted it on. Give someone `contribute` on a Program and they get it on
every Project and Task in that Program.

Mark an item **public** to workspace members, or **private**, where only holders of an explicit
grant see it at all.

Define your own roles per workspace: a nonprofit names one "Volunteer", an agency names one
"Client". Manage all of this in **Settings → Members & Access**.

## Switching, adding, and leaving

Switch and create workspaces from the switcher at the top of the sidebar. Leave one and it drops
out of your Hub while the workspace carries on for everyone else.
