Docket connects to the tools your work already lives in, one workspace at a time, so a workspace
can hold real tasks and deadlines from the first morning.

## Connections and imports

Docket keeps these two separate, on separate screens.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="arrows-rotate">
    An **ongoing sync**. Docket keeps reading the source and reflecting changes as they happen.
    Set up in **Settings → Connections**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import" icon="box-archive">
    A **one-time move**. Everything from another tool comes across once, and the source is then
    done. Set up in **Settings → Import**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Use Import when you are migrating off a tool. Use Connections when you intend to keep using both.

## What you can connect

| Tool | What it brings in |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Calendar** | Your events, as ordinary rows on Docket's calendar. Scheduled work writes back. |
| **Google Tasks** | Your existing tasks. |
| **Gmail** | Mail that implies work, which Athena turns into task suggestions for you to accept. |
| **Google Drive** | Files, so they can be attached to work and found in search. |
| **Linear** | Issues, projects, and cycles. |
| **GitHub** | Issues and pull requests, linkable onto Docket work. |
| **Notion** | A two-way mirror of designated databases. |

A connector appears as available only when it is genuinely connectable in your deployment. Docket
marks a tool whose credentials are not configured as unavailable instead of offering a connection
that would fail.

## Personal connections and workspace connections

**Personal connections** live in **Settings → Connections**, under the Personal group. They are
the data sources Athena reads on your behalf. Your Gmail and your calendar are yours, and no one
else in a workspace sees them.

**Workspace connections** live in **Settings → Connections** inside a shared workspace. An admin
manages them, and everyone in that workspace shares them. A Linear connection belongs to the team
itself.

Your personal space has no separate workspace connections, because its data sources are already
your personal ones.

## Google Calendar, specifically

The calendar connection runs in both directions. Events from Google appear on Docket's calendar,
and work you schedule in Docket is written back to Google so the rest of your life sees it.

Docket preserves the source calendar's time zone, so an event keeps its intended time when your
own time zone differs.

See [Calendar and scheduling](/guides/daily/calendar-and-scheduling) for what happens once the
events are in.

## Gmail, specifically

Mail is the input to Athena's task suggestions. She reads a message, decides what work it implies,
and drafts a Task with the original email attached as context. Every suggestion waits for you to
accept, edit, or dismiss it.

Turn this on and tune its rules under **Settings → Connections → Email to task**. See
[Athena and your mail](/guides/athena/athena-and-your-mail).

## When a connection breaks

Access tokens expire and get revoked. When that happens, Docket marks the connection as needing
re-authorization and notifies the owner. A connection never reports success while doing nothing,
and Docket records and displays every failure.

Re-authorize from the same Connections screen.
