Connect tools one workspace at a time.

## Connections and imports

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Docket keeps reading the source and reflects changes as they happen. Set one up in
    **Settings → Connections**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Import" icon="box-archive">
    Docket copies everything across once and then leaves the source alone. Run one from
    **Settings → Import**.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Migrating off a tool is an Import. Keeping both tools running is a Connection.

## What you can connect

| Tool | What it brings in |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Calendar** | Your events, as ordinary rows on Docket's calendar. Scheduled work writes back. |
| **Google Tasks** | Your existing tasks. |
| **Gmail** | Mail that implies work, which Athena turns into task suggestions for you to accept. |
| **Google Drive** | Files you can attach to work and find in search. |
| **Linear** | Issues, projects, and cycles. |
| **GitHub** | Issues and pull requests, linkable onto Docket work. |
| **Notion** | A two-way mirror of designated databases. |

Docket marks a tool whose credentials your deployment has not configured as unavailable.

## Personal connections and workspace connections

Connect **your own** Gmail and calendar under the Personal group in **Settings → Connections**.
Athena reads them on your behalf. Nobody else in a workspace sees them.

An admin connects **shared** tools from **Settings → Connections** inside the workspace, and
everyone there uses them. Linear belongs to the team.

Your personal space has no separate workspace connections.

## Google Calendar, specifically

The connection runs both ways. Google's events appear on Docket's calendar, and Docket writes work
you schedule back to Google.

Docket preserves the source calendar's time zone. An event keeps its intended time when your own
time zone differs.

See [Calendar and scheduling](/guides/daily/calendar-and-scheduling) for what happens once the
events are in.

## Gmail, specifically

Athena reads a message, works out what it implies, and drafts a Task with the original email
attached. Accept, edit, or dismiss each suggestion.

Turn it on and tune its rules under **Settings → Connections → Email to task**. See
[Athena and your mail](/guides/athena/athena-and-your-mail).

## When a connection breaks

Access tokens expire and get revoked. Docket marks the connection as needing re-authorization,
notifies the owner, and records the failure; a connection never reports success while doing
nothing. Re-authorize from the same Connections screen.
