Signing up takes three or four screens depending on the path you pick. You can change everything
chosen here later.

## 1. Sign in

Docket has no passwords. You sign in with a **passkey**, which can be Face ID, Touch ID, or a
security key, or with an account you already have from a supported provider.

If you sign up through a provider, Docket offers to add a passkey at the end of onboarding. Adding
one takes a moment and makes every later sign-in a single gesture. You can add one at any time
from **Settings → Security**.

## 2. Choose what brings you here

The first question decides the shape of your first workspace.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Just me">
    A personal space to run your own work and bring everything together. Docket creates it for
    you, so there is no name to invent and no roster to set up.
  </Card>
  <Card title="My team or company">
    A shared workspace for a startup or growing team to plan and ship together.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A nonprofit">
    A workspace for mission-driven work: programs, campaigns, and the people behind them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The two shared options ask you to name the workspace on the next screen. You can edit that name
later in **Settings → General**.

Your choice also sets the workspace's starting
[terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology). A nonprofit sees Campaigns, Seasons, and Chapters
where a startup sees Initiatives, Cycles, and Teams. Same objects, different labels.

<Note>
  Picking "Just me" still leaves the other options open. You can create a shared workspace at any
  time, and your personal space stays where it is. Most people end up with both.
</Note>

## 3. Bring in your work

The last step connects the tools your work already lives in, so the workspace holds real tasks and
deadlines the moment you arrive.

Connect as many as you like, or skip and start fresh. The button says "Continue without
connecting" until something has been brought in, then changes to "Enter your workspace".

See [Connect your calendar and mail](/guides/connect-calendar-and-mail) for what each connection
brings in and what it keeps in sync.

## What you see first

<Steps>

  <Step title="Athena">
    Ask her something in plain language. "What should I focus on today?" is a fair first question.

  </Step>

## Adding more workspaces later

Use the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar. Creating a workspace opens a focused page
that asks for a name and a terminology preset, then opens the workspace when it is ready.

Every workspace you belong to appears in your Hub automatically. You never sign in separately for
each one, and [Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio) shows all of them on one timeline.
