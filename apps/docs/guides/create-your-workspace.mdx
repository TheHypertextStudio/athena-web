## 1. Sign in

Docket has no passwords. Sign in with a **passkey**, using Face ID, Touch ID, or a security key,
or with an account from a supported provider. Add a passkey later from **Settings → Security**.

## 2. Pick personal or shared

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Just me">
    Docket creates a personal space for you. No name to invent, no roster.
  </Card>
  <Card title="My team or company">
    Name a shared workspace, then invite people into it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A nonprofit">
    Name a shared workspace that starts on nonprofit
    [terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology): Campaigns, Seasons, Chapters.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Your choice sets the workspace's starting terminology. Rename the workspace under
**Settings → General**.

<Note>
  Start with "Just me". Creating shared workspaces later does not remove your personal space.
</Note>

## 3. Connect one tool

Connect a calendar first. Docket puts your events on its calendar as ordinary rows and writes work
you schedule back to Google. Add Gmail, Google Tasks, Drive, Linear, GitHub, or Notion beside it.

See [Connect your calendar and mail](/guides/connect-calendar-and-mail) for what each one brings
in and what it syncs.

## Your first day

Open [Home](/guides/daily/home-today). Drag three tasks onto today and give each one a duration.
Ask Athena "What should I focus on today?" and she drafts the rest for you to approve. See
[What Athena is](/guides/athena/what-athena-is).

With nothing connected, create one Project for something you are doing this month and work from
there.

## Adding more workspaces later

Create one from the workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar. Docket adds it to your Hub and
shows it on the [Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio) timeline beside the rest.
