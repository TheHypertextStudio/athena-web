Docket's calendar holds your meetings and your work in one grid. Any task with a duration sits on
it the same way an event does, so your plan and your week stay a single picture.

## Layers

Each source of time is a layer you can show, hide, and share on its own:

- Your Google calendar
- A workspace's shared schedule
- Your own scheduled work

Share a layer with other people and they see the shape of your week without seeing the contents of
every event in it.

## Two-way Google sync

Connect Google Calendar and its events appear as ordinary rows here. Docket writes the work you
schedule back to Google, so the rest of your life sees the time you have committed.

Every event keeps its source calendar's time zone.

See [Connect your calendar and mail](/guides/connect-calendar-and-mail).

## Scheduling a week

Docket can lay out a week from the work you have and the time you have free.

Kinds of time differ. Filming a session, sitting in a community meeting, writing something long,
reading on a bus, and debriefing after an event each need different conditions. Docket models the
kind of time each open block offers and places work into time that suits it, so a three-hour shoot
never lands in a 25-minute bus ride.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimates come first">
    Scheduling reads each task's [time estimate](/guides/concepts/tasks). Docket cannot place work
    that has no estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your real calendar sets the limits">
    Existing meetings and events stay fixed. Scheduling fills the time around them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You approve the week">
    The result arrives as a proposal you accept, edit, or reject, under the same
    [approval model](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals) everything else uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next week's estimates improve">
    The [time ledger](/guides/daily/time) records how long work really took, and scheduling reads
    that history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The daily loop

Weekly scheduling sets the shape of the week. Each morning, [Home](/guides/daily/home-today)
adjusts that shape against what actually happened the day before.

## Scheduling defaults

Working hours, default durations, and calendar sharing live in **Settings → Calendar**.
