Give a task a duration and Docket adds it to the calendar next to your 10am standup.

## Layers

Use layers to show and hide each source of time:

- Your Google calendar
- A workspace's shared schedule
- Your own scheduled work

Share a layer and the other person sees your busy blocks without the contents of any event.

## Google Calendar sync

Connect Google Calendar and Docket writes the work you schedule back to it.

Docket shows every event in its source calendar's time zone.

See [Connect your calendar and mail](/guides/connect-calendar-and-mail).

## Schedule a week

Docket schedules your week from the estimates on your tasks and the gaps between your meetings. It
matches the kind of time each gap offers and never schedules a three-hour shoot into a 25-minute
bus ride.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Estimate every task first">
    Scheduling reads the [time estimate](/guides/concepts/tasks). A task with no estimate never
    gets placed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meetings win">
    Docket moves no existing event. Scheduling uses the time between them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve the week">
    Docket shows the week as a proposal. Accept it, edit it, or reject it, under the same
    [approval model](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals) everything else uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let the estimates correct themselves">
    The [time ledger](/guides/daily/time) records how long the work took, and next week's
    scheduling reads that history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each morning, [Home](/guides/daily/home-today) reschedules the rest of the week from what happened
yesterday.

## Scheduling defaults

Set working hours, default durations, and calendar sharing in **Settings → Calendar**.
