Drag a task onto today from any workspace you are a member of. Home is one of your Hub screens. One
plan shows your startup's tasks next to your nonprofit's, and each row names the workspace it came
from.

Add a task to today and nothing about the task changes: not its status, not its project, not who it
is assigned to. The plan is yours.

## Fit the plan to your hours

Give every task a
[duration](/guides/concepts/tasks#fill-in-the-time-estimate), add the plan up against
the hours your calendar leaves free, then cut until the two numbers agree. You find out at 9am when
the day does not fit.

Docket shows your meetings and scheduled work beside the plan on the calendar.

## The prompt box

Type a task and Home captures it. Ask a question and [Athena](/guides/athena/what-athena-is)
answers it. Same input.

Ask her things like:

- "What should I focus on today?"
- "Plan my day around the two calls I already have."
- "What's blocking the Spring Gala project?"
- "Create a plan to make sure I get more sleep."

Athena reads the work you can see and proposes changes for you to approve. See
[Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## Blockers and approvals

Blockers appear inline, beside the task they block. Approve a paused agent session from Home
without opening the session.

## Planning the week

Docket schedules a whole week from your durations and the hours you have. See
[Calendar and scheduling](/guides/daily/calendar-and-scheduling#schedule-a-week).
