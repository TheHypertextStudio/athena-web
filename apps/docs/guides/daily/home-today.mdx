Home is the screen you open in the morning. It belongs to your Hub, so it spans every workspace
you are a member of.

Three things sit side by side:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Today's plan" icon="list-check">
    The tasks you have chosen for today, pulled from any workspace, each carrying its time
    estimate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your calendar" icon="calendar">
    The day's real shape — meetings, events, and any work you have scheduled.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Needs attention" icon="bell">
    Agent approvals, blockers, and things coming due.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Building the plan

Drag a task in from anywhere. Every item wears a chip naming the workspace it came from, so work
from four different ventures can sit in one list without any of it getting confused.

The plan is **yours**. Adding a task to today changes nothing about the task itself — not its
status, not its project, not who it is assigned to. You are recording an intention about your own
day.

### Durations make it honest

Each task in the plan shows its [time estimate](/guides/concepts/tasks#the-time-estimate-is-the-important-one).
Set against the hours actually free on your calendar, that is what turns a list of intentions into
a plan you can believe.

A day that does not fit is worth knowing about at 9am.

## The prompt box

There is one input at the top of Home that takes both. Type a task and it captures one. Ask a
question and [Athena](/guides/athena/what-athena-is) answers it.

Things worth asking:

- "What should I focus on today?"
- "Plan my day around the two calls I already have."
- "What's blocking the Spring Gala project?"
- "Create a plan to make sure I get more sleep."

Athena reads the same work you can see, and proposes rather than acts. See
[Proposals and approvals](/guides/athena/proposals-and-approvals).

## Blockers appear where the work is

Anything standing in your way shows up **inline, beside the task it is blocking**, rather than
collected into a separate bucket you have to remember to open.

The same is true of agent approvals: a session waiting on your sign-off appears here as well as
in the session itself, so you can approve from wherever you happen to be.

## Home and Inbox

They are neighbours doing different jobs.

| | Home | [Inbox](/guides/daily/inbox) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Answers | "What am I doing today?" | "What needs a response from me?" |
| Contents | Work you chose | Things that arrived |
| Emptying it means | The day is planned | Nothing is waiting on you |
