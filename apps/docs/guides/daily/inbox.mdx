title: "Inbox"
description: "Everything waiting on a response from you, across every workspace, in one queue."
---

Work the Inbox when you owe people replies. It queues what needs an action from you, from every
workspace at once.

| Row type | Why it arrived |
| --- | --- |
| **Mentions** | Someone named you in a comment, an update, or a description. |
| **Assignments** | Someone assigned work to you. |
| **Agent approvals** | A session is paused waiting for your sign-off. |
| **Replies** | Discussion continued on something you are part of. |
| **Due and overdue** | Work with a date that has arrived. |

Read the row: "Dani replied on your project", "you were assigned a pull request". Decide from it.
Send anything that will take real work to [today's plan](/guides/daily/home-today), and approve a
paused agent session without leaving the queue.

Empty the Inbox and nothing is waiting on you.

## Activity

Nothing in the activity feed needs you. It records what happened across Docket and your connected
tools in one shape: Docket shows a Docket task, a Linear issue, and a GitHub pull request in the
same row, each with a badge naming where it came from.

## Notifications

Choose email, SMS, push, or web delivery in **Settings → Notifications**, and set your contact
points there. Turn a channel off and Docket notifies you on the ones you left on. Your Inbox does
not change.
