---
title: 'Inbox and Stream'
description: 'Act on notifications in Inbox, and use Stream to follow the full history of work.'
---

Inbox holds notifications that concern you. Stream records what happened across your workspaces.
Use Inbox to respond. Use Stream when you need context.

## Work the Inbox

Inbox separates **Needs action** from ordinary **Updates**. Use the tabs to show everything,
unread items, requests that need action, announcements, mentions and assignments, or recent
activity.

Approve a supported request from its row. Open other notifications to act in their source. Mark an
item as read when you have handled it, or use **Mark all read** for the current feed.

Every cross-workspace row names the workspace that produced it.

## Use Stream for the full history

The personal Stream shows events across every workspace you can access. A workspace Stream limits
the same timeline to that workspace.

Stream groups related events into episodes instead of showing each field change as an isolated
notification. Select an event to open its details. Use **Filters**, **Group**, and **Sort** to narrow
the timeline. A banner lets you reveal new events without moving your reading position.

Connected tools can add events to Stream. Each event keeps its source and links back to the object
that produced it.

## Set notification delivery

Choose the available email, SMS, push, and web channels under **Settings → Notifications**.
Changing a delivery channel does not remove the notification from Inbox.
