Inbox is the cross-workspace queue of things that need you. Mentions, comments on your work,
assignments, agent approvals, and items coming due all arrive here regardless of which workspace
they came from.

It is a Hub surface, so there is exactly one and it spans everything.

## What lands here

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Mentions** | Someone named you in a comment, an update, or a description. |
| **Assignments** | Work was handed to you. |
| **Agent approvals** | A session is paused waiting for your sign-off. |
| **Replies** | Discussion continued on something you are part of. |
| **Due and overdue** | Work with a date that has arrived. |

Every row carries a chip naming its workspace.

## Inbox and the activity feed

These are deliberately separate, and the difference is whether you have to do something.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Inbox" icon="inbox">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Activity" icon="waveform">
    Passive awareness — what happened, across every connected tool. Nothing here is waiting on
    you.
  </Card>

The activity feed shows events from Docket and from your connected tools in one shape. A Docket
task, a Linear issue, and a GitHub pull request are all "a piece of work" and render through one
row with a small badge for where they came from, so there are no separate layouts to learn.

## Working through it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the row, not the item">
    Rows say what happened in plain language — "Dani replied on your project", "you were assigned
    a pull request". Most of the time that is enough to decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act, or send it to today">
    Anything that will take real work belongs in [today's plan](/guides/daily/home-today) rather
    than in a half-read inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve agent work here">
    A pending approval can be actioned from the Inbox without opening the session, though opening
    it shows you what the agent actually did.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inbox zero

Docket treats an empty inbox as a real state with its own screen, rather than a blank list. Empty
means nothing is waiting on you — which is information, and worth showing as such.

## Notifications

What reaches you outside the app — email, SMS, push, and the web — is configured separately in
**Settings → Notifications**, described there as deciding what Athena tells you, and where.

Your contact points live in the same place. Turning a channel down changes where you are told,
leaving what lands in your Inbox unchanged.
