Portfolio is the cross-workspace roadmap. Every venture you are part of, and everything ongoing or
bounded inside it, sits on one shared timeline.

It is a Hub surface. Workspaces stay separated in the data and are gathered here in the view.

## What it shows

Portfolio lists each workspace, then the Programs and Projects inside it, each with its dates and
its [health](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health).

One screen answers the questions that otherwise take four browser tabs:

- What is every venture actually working on right now?
- What is `at_risk` anywhere?
- What lands in the same fortnight, across two organizations that do not know about each other?
- What has gone quiet?

## Health at a glance

Every row carries its health chip, `on_track`, `at_risk`, or `off_track`, set by the
[updates](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health) posted on the item.

An Initiative shows health rolled up from what sits beneath it, so a theme going wrong is visible
without opening anything.

Initiatives also raise a **stale** flag when nobody has posted an update in long enough that the
health signal can no longer be trusted, so an absent update never passes for a good one.

## Spotting collisions between ventures

Running more than one organization hides collisions until they get expensive. The gala and the
launch can both be healthy, both need your whole week, and neither workspace knows the other
exists. One timeline shows you the overlap while you can still act on it.

## Dropping in

Selecting anything takes you into its workspace, where the app rebinds. It speaks that workspace's
[terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology) and shows its people and its tools.

Step back to the Hub whenever you want every workspace on screen again.

## Portfolio and cycles

Portfolio works at the scale of months. For what a team is doing over the next week or two, use
[Cycles](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones) inside a workspace. A project's work list
defaults to the current cycle.
