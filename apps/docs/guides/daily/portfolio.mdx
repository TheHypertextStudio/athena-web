Portfolio is the cross-workspace roadmap. Every venture you are part of, and everything ongoing or
bounded inside it, on one shared timeline.

It is a Hub surface. Workspaces stay separated in the data and are gathered here in the view.

## What it shows

Portfolio reads vertically: each workspace, then the Programs and Projects inside it, each with
its dates and its [health](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health).

That means one screen answers the questions that otherwise take four browser tabs:

- What is every venture actually working on right now?
- What is `at_risk` anywhere?
- What lands in the same fortnight, across two organizations that do not know about each other?
- What has gone quiet?

## Health at a glance

Every row carries its health chip — `on_track`, `at_risk`, or `off_track` — driven by the
[updates](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health) posted on it.

An Initiative shows health rolled up from what sits beneath it, so a theme going wrong is visible
without opening anything.

Watch for **stale**: an item nobody has updated in long enough that the health signal can no
longer be trusted. Silence reads as "fine" in most tools, and Portfolio deliberately declines to
let it.

## The collision this surface exists for

Running more than one organization means the collisions are invisible until they are expensive.
The gala and the launch are both fine, and both need your whole week, and no single workspace was
ever going to tell you.

Seeing them on one timeline is the point.

## Dropping in

Selecting anything takes you into its workspace, at which point the app rebinds — it speaks that
workspace's [terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology) and shows its people and its tools.

Step back to the Hub whenever you want the wide view again.

## Portfolio and cycles

Portfolio is the strategic altitude, measured in months. For what a team is doing over the next
week or two, use [Cycles](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones) inside a workspace — a project's
work list defaults to the current cycle.
