title: "Portfolio"

---

Portfolio puts every Program and Project you have, across every workspace, on one timeline with its
dates and its [health](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health).

Use it to answer:

- What is `at_risk` anywhere?
- What lands in the same fortnight, across two organizations that do not know about each other?
- What has gone quiet?

## Health at a glance

Read each row's health chip: `on_track`, `at_risk`, or `off_track`, set by the
[updates](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health) posted on the item. An Initiative shows health
rolled up from what sits beneath it.

Initiatives also raise a **stale** flag when nobody has posted an update in long enough to trust
the health signal.

## Collisions between ventures

The gala and the launch can both be healthy, both need your whole week, and neither workspace knows
the other exists. One timeline shows you the overlap while you can still move something.

## Dropping in

Select anything to enter its workspace. The app rebinds to that workspace's
[terminology](/guides/concepts/terminology), people, and tools.

Portfolio works at the scale of months. For the next week or two, use
[Cycles](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones) inside a workspace.
