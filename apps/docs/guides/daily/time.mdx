---
title: 'Time'
description: 'Run a timer while you work and review your private work history afterward.'
---

Docket records time from timers, past-time entries, and agent sessions. The Time page is your
private work history. Other members do not get a copy of your personal review.

## Start a timer where the work is

Start a task timer from the task, Today, Calendar, or the **Focus** panel in the utility rail.
Pause, resume, or stop it from Focus without leaving the page you are using.

Starting a different timer closes the current interval before it opens the next one. Docket does
not infer time from a status change or a calendar event.

## Add time after the fact

Select **Add past time** when you forgot to start a timer. Choose the date, start and end times,
workspace, and optional Project, Task, or category.

Open a session in the history to edit or remove it when the record allows changes.

## Choose the period

Review a **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Cycle**, or **Custom** range. Use the arrows to move the
selected period backward or forward.

The page stores the period and filters in the URL. You can return to the same review or open it on
another device.

## Filter the records

Use **Filters** to narrow the history by workspace, Project, Task, category, capture source, or
other available fields. Active filters appear above the results and can be removed one at a time.

## Compare human and agent effort

Choose **Human effort**, **Agent effort**, or **Combined effort**. Then use **Break down by** to
group the total by:

- Workspace
- Project
- Task
- Category
- Capture source

Select a breakdown row to open the sessions behind that total. Docket keeps the total and the
underlying records connected, so you can check where a number came from.
