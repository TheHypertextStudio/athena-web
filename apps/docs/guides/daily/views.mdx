Views keep a useful arrangement of work so you do not rebuild it every time.

## Change a work view

Use the controls above Tasks, Projects, Programs, or Initiatives:

- **Filter** limits which items appear.
- **Group** collects items under a field such as status or assignee.
- **Sort** controls their order.
- **Display** changes visible properties and presentation details.

The available layouts fit the work type. Tasks, Projects, Programs, and Initiatives can use List,
Board, and Cards. Projects and Initiatives can also use Timeline.

## Save the setup

Open **Views** in the workspace sidebar to create and manage saved views. A View records its work
type, layout, filters, grouping, sorting, and display settings.

Save one after the arrangement answers a question you expect to ask again. For example, save
Projects grouped by health and filtered to one Initiative. Reset the working View when you want to
return to its saved state.

Personal Views belong to you. Shared Views use workspace access rules.
