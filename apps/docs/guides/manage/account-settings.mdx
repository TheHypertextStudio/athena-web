---
title: 'Personal settings'
description: 'Manage your account, notifications, calendar, security, and Docket data.'
---

The Personal group in Settings belongs to your account. It does not change when you switch the
workspace shown below it.

## Profile and Athena

**Profile** holds your name, email, and personal identity. **Athena** sets your standing
instructions and normal approval mode. Workspace and Team instructions remain separate from these
personal preferences.

## Connections and connected apps

**Connections** links the tools your personal workspace uses. **Connected accounts** manages the
external identities those connections use. **Connected apps** lists outside applications that can
access Docket through your authorization.

Revoking a connected app deletes its consent and access tokens. The app must ask you to authorize
again before it can return.

## Notifications

Open **Notifications** to choose channels, quiet hours, and the events that should reach you. Web,
email, SMS, and push appear only where the deployment can provide them.

## Calendar and work locations

**Calendar** controls what scheduled work creates and what coworkers can see. **Work locations**
stores named places and repeating location schedules. A connected account can supply location
context without changing who can read your calendar event.

## Security

Open **Security** to manage passkeys, recovery codes, and signed-in sessions. Store recovery codes
away from the device you normally use to sign in.

## Export or delete data

Open **Data & privacy** to request an export or delete Docket data. An export is prepared in the
background and keeps workspace boundaries in its manifest. Deletion requires a separate confirmed
action because it cannot be undone.
