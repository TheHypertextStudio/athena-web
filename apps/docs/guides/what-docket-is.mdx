Docket is one tool for planning, scheduling, and tracking work. A task carries a duration, lands
on your calendar, and logs the hours it actually took, so the plan, the week, and the time are one
record.

It holds every organization you belong to at once. A startup, a nonprofit, a client roster, and
your own personal work all live in the same app, each in its own separate world, all visible from
one place that belongs to you.

## The two altitudes

Everything in Docket sits at one of two heights, and knowing which one you are standing at
explains most of the product.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your Hub" icon="house">
    Yours alone. There is exactly one per person. It gathers work from every organization you
    belong to and lays it out together — today's plan, everything awaiting your response, and one
    timeline across all your ventures. Every item wears a chip naming the organization it came
    from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A workspace" icon="building">
    A shared, isolated world. Its members, its connected tools, its agents, its terminology, and
    all of its work are scoped to it. Drop into one and the whole app rebinds to that context.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Data never merges across that boundary. Views aggregate. Your nonprofit's donor outreach stays
separate from your startup's fundraising push, and you can still see both on one screen this
morning.

## What you plan with

Inside any workspace, work is described with a small vocabulary. Each word answers a different
question about the shape of the effort.

| Word | What it describes | Does it finish? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | The atomic unit — the single thing someone does. | Optional due date |
| **Project** | A bounded effort with an outcome. "Launch v2", "Spring Gala". | Yes |
| **Program** | An ongoing area of operations. "Customer Support", "After-School Program". | Usually runs on |
| **Initiative** | A strategic theme that gathers programs and projects. "Grow revenue". | No |
| **Cycle** | A recurring stretch of time a team commits work to. | Yes, fixed |
| **Team** | A group of people that owns its own workflow, cycles, and triage queue. | — |

Two of these are worth flagging early, because most tools lack them.

**Program** is the home for work that keeps going after each deliverable inside it lands. Projects
end and themes hold no work, which leaves continuous day-after-day operations homeless. Program
is where it lives.

**Cycle** schedules Tasks without containing them. The same Task can be pulled into a cycle,
handed to a teammate, or flagged as blocked, and none of that changes what kind of work it is.

[Core concepts](/guides/concepts/workspaces) walks through each of these properly.

## People and agents are the same kind of thing

Everywhere Docket asks "who" — assignee, owner, the author of a change — it points at one concept
called an **Actor**. An Actor is a person or an AI agent, and the two are interchangeable in the
assignee field. You hand a task to a colleague or to an agent with the same move, in the same
place.

Docket's own agent is called **Athena**. Delegating to her opens a session: a live, plain-English
account of what she is reading, deciding, and about to do. She pauses for your approval before
committing anything that matters, and every action she takes records both names — the agent that
did it and the person who set it in motion.

[Athena](/guides/athena/what-athena-is) covers this in full.
