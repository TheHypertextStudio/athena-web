title: "What Docket is"
description: "One tool for planning, scheduling, and tracking every kind of work across every organization you belong to."
---

Docket plans, schedules, and tracks work in one place. A task carries a duration, lands on your
calendar, and logs the hours it actually took, so the plan, the week, and the time stay in one
record.

Docket holds every organization you belong to at once. A startup, a nonprofit, a client roster,
and your own personal work each stay separate, and you see all of them from one place that belongs
to you.

## The two levels

Everything in Docket sits at one of two levels.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your Hub" icon="house">
    Yours alone, and there is exactly one per person. It gathers work from every organization you
    belong to: today's plan, everything awaiting your response, and one timeline across all your
    ventures. Every item shows a chip naming the organization it came from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A workspace" icon="building">
    A shared, isolated organization. Its members, its connected tools, its agents, its
    terminology, and all of its work are scoped to it. Open one and the whole app switches to that
    context.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Data never merges across that boundary. Hub views gather it for display. Your nonprofit's donor
outreach stays separate from your startup's fundraising push, and you can still see both on one
screen this morning.

## What you plan with

Inside any workspace, a small vocabulary describes work. Each word answers a different question
about the shape of the effort.

| Word | What it describes | Does it finish? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | The atomic unit. The single thing someone does. | Optional due date |
| **Project** | A bounded effort with an outcome. "Launch v2", "Spring Gala". | Yes |
| **Program** | An ongoing area of operations. "Customer Support", "After-School Program". | Usually runs on |
| **Initiative** | A strategic theme that gathers programs and projects. "Grow revenue". | No |
| **Cycle** | A recurring stretch of time a team commits work to. | Yes, fixed |
| **Team** | A group of people that owns its own workflow, cycles, and triage queue. | Not applicable |

**Program** holds work that keeps going after each deliverable inside it lands. Projects end and
Initiatives hold no work, so continuous day-after-day operations belong to a Program.

**Cycle** schedules Tasks without containing them. The same Task can be pulled into a cycle,
handed to a teammate, or flagged as blocked, and none of that changes what kind of work it is.

[Core concepts](/guides/concepts/workspaces) covers each of these in full.

## People and agents are the same kind of thing

Everywhere Docket asks who is responsible (assignee, owner, the author of a change) it points at
one concept called an **Actor**. An Actor is a person or an AI agent, and the two are
interchangeable in the assignee field. You hand a task to a colleague or to an agent with the same
move, in the same place.

Docket's own agent is called **Athena**. Delegating to her opens a session: a live, plain-English
account of what she is reading, deciding, and about to do. She pauses for your approval before
committing anything that matters, and every action she takes records two names, the agent that did
it and the person who set it in motion.

[Athena](/guides/athena/what-athena-is) covers this in full.
