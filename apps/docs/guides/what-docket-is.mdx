---
title: "What Docket is"
description: "One place to plan, schedule, and track work across every organization you belong to."
---

Give a task a duration. Docket schedules it on your calendar and logs the hours it took against
the same record.

Docket holds every organization you belong to at once. Your startup and your nonprofit stay
separate, and you work on both from one place that belongs to you.

## Your Hub reads across every workspace

Docket never merges data across that boundary. Your Hub reads from both sides and puts your
nonprofit's donor outreach on the screen beside your startup's fundraising push.

## The vocabulary

Six words describe work inside a workspace.

| Word | What it describes | Does it finish? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | Call the caterer. One person does it. | Optional due date |
| **Project** | Spring Gala. Launch v2. You close it when it ships. | Yes |
| **Program** | Use a Program for work that never finishes, like customer support or bookkeeping. | Usually runs on |
| **Initiative** | Group programs and projects under an Initiative like "Grow revenue". It holds no tasks. | No |
| **Cycle** | Use Cycles to plan work in two-week sprints. | Yes, fixed |
| **Team** | Support, or the Berlin chapter. Each team owns its statuses, cycles, and triage queue. | Not applicable |

[Core concepts](/guides/concepts/workspaces) covers each of these in full.

## People and agents

Assign a task to a colleague or to an AI agent in the same field. Docket calls both **Actors**.

Assign one to Athena and watch her work. She opens a session that says in plain English what she
is reading and about to do, and she waits for your approval before committing anything that
matters.

[Athena](/guides/athena/what-athena-is) covers this in full.
