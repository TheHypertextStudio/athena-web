## Every morning: build the plan

Open [Home](/guides/daily/home-today).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add what matters to today">
    Drag tasks from any workspace onto today. Each one shows the workspace it came from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give each one a duration">
    Give every task a duration so your day fits. Three tasks at 45 minutes against two free hours
    is a number you want before lunch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear what is blocking you">
    Docket shows blockers, agent approvals, and overdue items inline in the plan. There is no
    separate queue to visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or ask Athena">
    Ask "What should I focus on today?" She reads the same work you can see and drafts a plan you
    approve or edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Through the day: do the work

Docket puts scheduled work on your [calendar](/guides/daily/calendar-and-scheduling) as ordinary
rows beside your meetings. Start a timer on a task and Docket logs the interval to the
[time ledger](/guides/daily/time).

Your [Inbox](/guides/daily/inbox) lists mentions, comments, and agents waiting on approval from
every workspace you belong to.

## Through the week: cycles and triage

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cycles set the cadence">
    Use Cycles to plan work in recurring sprints. Each team picks its length, commonly two weeks,
    and cycles roll forward on their own. See
    [Cycles and milestones](/guides/concepts/cycles-and-milestones).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Triage lists unfiled work">
    A Linear issue that arrived overnight waits in Triage until you file it. So does anything you
    captured in a hurry. A task is allowed to have no project.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## End of the week: post an update

Write a paragraph on the project you moved saying how it is going, and set its health. Everyone
reading [Portfolio](/guides/daily/portfolio) gets that verdict without opening anything. See
[Updates and health](/guides/concepts/updates-and-health).

Then read the [time ledger](/guides/daily/time) against what you planned and adjust your estimates.
Athena can write the retrospective for you.

## Day by day

| Day | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Connect a calendar and one work tool. Docket copies your existing work in. |
| 1 | Create one Project for something you are doing this month. |
| 2 | Plan a morning from Home. Give three tasks a duration and compare the total to your free hours. |
| 3 | Delegate one task to Athena and watch the session. Approve or reject what she proposes. |
| 4 | Post an update on the Project. Notice the health signal change in Portfolio. |
| 5 | Read the time ledger against the plan. Adjust your estimates. |
