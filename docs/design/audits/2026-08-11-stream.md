## Gate evidence

- **A11y:** The 320px journey verifies a 40px filter touch target, visible focus after keyboard modality, Enter activation, Escape dismissal, semantic main/heading/article/list/dialog roles, and unique drawer dismissal labels.
- **Responsive:** The functional journey asserts zero document overflow at 390px and 320px; all four screenshots show intentional desktop/mobile reflow.
- **Theme parity:** The complete 1440×900 and 390×844 frames were captured and inspected in both light and dark themes.
- **No placeholder:** The screenshots use events created through the real local API. Filters, disclosure, exact-event drawer, retry, and empty-state actions are implemented and exercised.
- **Screenshot-verified:** Every visual observation above is visible in the four archived screenshots.

## Findings (ordered by severity)

1. Resolved during review: the shared filter trigger inherited insufficient dark-theme text contrast at mobile width. Stream now requests an always-visible label, and the trigger applies direct semantic foreground tokens — `apps/web/src/components/views/add-filter-menu.tsx:45`.
2. Resolved during review: due-date deltas exposed raw ISO dates and PATCH state events omitted their actual prior state. Details now use a compact human date, and the API records the real before/after transition — `apps/web/src/components/stream/stream-meta.ts`, `apps/api/src/routes/tasks.ts`.
3. Resolved during review: the backdrop and close button originally shared the same accessible name. The backdrop now identifies itself as dismissal while the explicit close button keeps its action label — `apps/web/src/components/stream/event-drawer.tsx`.

Verdict: **SHIP** — all eight dimensions meet the crafted bar and every hard gate is green.
