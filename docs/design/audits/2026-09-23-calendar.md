# Design review: Calendar — 2026-09-23

For Calendar maintainers: keep the measured date axis, compact overlap disclosure, and direct phone editor when changing this view. The captures use a throwaway fixture account with long titles, concurrent events, dense all-day events, work location, and cross-day events. The user's original screenshot contains private data and is not in the repository.

The [screenshot set](screenshots/2026-09-23-calendar-craft/) includes 1440×900, 768×844, 390×844, 320×844, and 390×600. Desktop and phone have light and dark captures. The 320, 390, and 768 px first-paint captures show the server HTML before JavaScript runs; the 768 px settled capture shows the two-day view after measurement. The set also includes the phone event editor, focused event cards, and a 32-document tab strip.

The accessibility, responsive, theme, no-placeholder, and screenshot gates pass for the captured states. Browser tests confirm one date lane at 320, 390, and 430 px, two at 768 px, and seven at 1440 px. The Calendar browser suite passes all 19 cases, including the server-rendered initial period, cross-day editing, phone detail, and provider interactions.

The later hour-gutter correction gives the compact 14 px time labels 56 px of space. The [320 px first frame](screenshots/2026-09-23-calendar-craft/calendar-320x844-first-paint.png) and [390 px first frame](screenshots/2026-09-23-calendar-craft/calendar-390x844-first-paint.png) show `12 PM` clear of the viewport edge. The [320 px measured grid](screenshots/2026-09-23-calendar-craft/calendar-320x844-time-gutter.png) and [390 px measured grid](screenshots/2026-09-23-calendar-craft/calendar-390x844-time-gutter.png) retain the inset after hydration. Browser assertions measure the rendered noon text, not just its containing box, and require at least 8 px of left clearance.

The seven-day desktop view still shortens titles when overlaps reduce a date lane to about 154 px. It shows one readable card and a nearby disclosure instead of three illegible columns. The full title and exact end time remain available through focus and the event workspace.

Verdict: **SHIP**. All eight dimensions score at least 3, and the five hard gates pass for the tested states.
